📣 Why the rules changed

In March 2026 the Eighth Circuit Court of Appeals struck down the SAVE plan, and the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of July 2025 already ordered SAVE, PAYE, and ICR to be phased out by July 1, 2028. Going forward, income-driven borrowers have exactly two choices: Income-Based Repayment (IBR), the old plan based on discretionary income, or the new Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP), based on a tiered percentage of total income.

Picking wrong can cost thousands per year or add a decade to your forgiveness timeline — so it pays to run the numbers first. 💡
